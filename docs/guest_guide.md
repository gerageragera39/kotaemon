# Guest guide

Guests use KURAGa through a restricted chat-only flow.

## What guests can do

1. Click **Access as Guest** on the welcome page.
2. Ask questions in **Chat**.
3. Read retrieved evidence/citations in the answer panel.
4. Open **Project Documentation** for scope, limitations, and attribution.
5. Log out.

## What guests cannot do

- Upload documents.
- Select one specific file.
- Disable document search.
- Use web-only search instead of the indexed university documents.
- Access Resources, Settings, Evaluation, or file-management/admin pages.

## What documents are searched?

Guest questions are forced to **Search All** over documents indexed by admins. In the default private file-collection setup, this means all documents owned by admin users.

## How to ask good questions

- Mention the programme, module, deadline, or rule you care about.
- Ask for the source or evidence when you need to verify details.
- Prefer focused questions such as “What are the admission requirements mentioned in the indexed documents?” over very broad prompts.
- If an answer is unclear, ask a follow-up question and compare the cited passages.

## Understanding citations and evidence

KURAGa retrieves document chunks before generating an answer. Citations/evidence show where the answer came from. They are useful for verification, but they do not guarantee the model interpreted the source correctly.

## Limitations

- The app only knows documents that admins have indexed.
- It can retrieve irrelevant chunks or miss relevant ones.
- The LLM can hallucinate or overstate uncertain information.
- Indexed documents may be outdated.
- KURAGa is not official KU guidance.
